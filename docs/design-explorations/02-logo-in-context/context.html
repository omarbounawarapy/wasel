What to look at

  • Sidebar: does the mark hold its own next to the wordmark at real size, and does it look like it belongs to the UI colours?
  • Tab and 16px: is the silhouette still recognisable?
  • Home screen and WhatsApp avatar: the mark sits in a navy tile or circle. Is it centred and readable, and does it look different from the icons around it?
