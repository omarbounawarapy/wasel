## What v2 fixed (versus v1)
- Plain tiers RISKY / UNSURE / SAFE were read correctly by all seven; nobody asked what they mean.
- Counts banner works: Youssef, Karim, Sarra and Mohamed all look at it first.
- Pre-filled draft with a big "SEND TO SAMI" key: named as a win by Mohamed, Ines, Karim, Youssef.
- French and Arabic screens noticed and liked by Amel, Ines, Rim.
- Trust rose for six of seven versus v1 (Sarra 4 to 8, Rim 2 to 5, Amel 3 to 4).

## Still wrong (ranked by how many personas said it)
1. The command box hint "TYPE A CODE OR TAP A KEY" says nothing: Youssef, Ines, Sarra, Karim (command line looks like code). Fix: "Enter an order number, or tap a key".
2. Codes and abbreviations: Amel (remove ORD/MSG/ADD/RES), Karim, Rim (jargon: mnemonics, tier vocabulary), Mohamed (CONFIRM, PREPAY, QUICK CHECK must be sentences). Fix: next steps as sentences ("Ask for the address"), codes only on desktop.
3. "SYNTHETIC DATA" everywhere reads as fake: Amel, Karim, Rim (blocks recommending), Sarra wants real history. It is required by the product, so show it once as a banner and in settings, not on every screen, and explain it in one sentence.
4. Technical desktop screens (D6, D5 detail) are noise for sellers: Karim, Youssef, Amel. Fix: hide behind "More" or a Technical team view; keep NOTHING IS WRONG.
5. Highlighted words and Arabizi are not explained in plain words: Mohamed, Amel earlier. Fix: add one sentence of meaning per highlight, offer Arabic-script draft first for readers.
6. Extra buttons (APPROVE, CANCEL) next to SEND: Ines, Rim ("which one stops the refusal?"). Fix: one primary key, others under "More".
7. Address check does not say what to do: Youssef, Karim, Rim. Fix: "ASK FOR IT" or "SAFE TO SEND" verdict line.
8. Help paragraph under the banner: Youssef. Fix: one line.
9. Desktop reply: edit inline (Sarra), filter by tier (Youssef), key shortcuts shown first (Ines), saved question (Ines).
10. Example product Casque Bluetooth is not typical Tunisian COD (Rim). Dataset is synthetic; consider clothes or cosmetics examples.

## Look
Dark amber terminal: Youssef, Ines, Sarra and Karim like it; Amel finds it intimidating ("hacker software") and Mohamed finds it tiring. The same split as v1, now with higher trust because the words are plain. Desktop users prefer it, phone-only users want a calmer phone surface.

## Misreadings to ignore
Sarra read INCERTAIN and SÛR as inconsistent with UNSURE and SAFE (they are the French glosses). Rim called French and Arabic "native" though I wrote them without a native check. Reports are role-played by a small model.

## Next if iterating (v3)
