# Persona test: Review Desk (04), simulated (haiku role-play, hypotheses not evidence)

| Persona | Trust | Price / month | Device | Main complaint |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amel | 6 | 75 TND after a 2 week trial | phone | "Why this score" is too much text; wants a huge send button; "Simulated" makes her doubt it is real |
| Karim | 7 | 35-50 TND | desktop | Orders and WhatsApp are two screens; cannot see what changed between score 87 and 16; wants keyboard shortcuts |
| Ines | 8 | 50-70 TND | desktop | Phone is useless for her; bars are noise; wants Tab/Enter, not J/K |
| Mohamed | 7 | 5-6 TND | phone | Highest / Elevated / Normal in English; "Arabizi" word makes him doubt; numbers too small |
| Sarra | 6 | 20 TND per client | desktop | Needs real data not a simulation; 71 point swing looks unstable; wants override notes and factor weights |
| Youssef | 8 | 250-300 TND | both | White is too bright on night shift; wants dark mode; mobile buttons too small |
| Rim | 7 | 300-400 TND | desktop | Wants real Darija examples; asks why the score was re-scored; replace bars with text |

Patterns: five of seven want the reason shown as plain text, not bars; Ines, Karim and Sarra ask for keyboard flow; Mohamed and Rim want Arabic or Darija; Youssef wants a dark theme. Trust is higher than for Terminal Desk v1 (4-8 vs 2-7 on the low end), and the light look was not rejected by anyone, but it does not win the dark-theme user. Caveat: these reports are role-played by a small model and some read the screens loosely.
