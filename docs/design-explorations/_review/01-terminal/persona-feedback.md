# Persona feedback on Terminal Desk (01), simulated clients

Seven role-played personas (small model, `haiku`) each saw the same saved screenshots in this folder and answered in a fixed format. These are simulated reactions, not real customers: treat them as hypotheses to test with real sellers. Price figures are not market evidence. Status: all seven back.

| Persona | Device | Trust | Would pay | One-line verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Amel, solo cosmetics seller, Sousse | phone | 3/10 | about 10 TND/month | "A computer for engineers, not for me" |
| Karim, electronics shop owner, Tunis | phone | 7/10 | 60 TND/month if it proves itself | "Reads your orders like a smart person and shows the money math" |
| Ines, order-handling assistant | desktop | 6/10 | boss pays 60 to 80 TND | "Make it smaller and stop trying to look like Bloomberg" |
| Mohamed, 58, shop owner, Kairouan | phone | not given | 150 to 200 TND if reliable | Needs Arabic, bigger text, plain words |
| Sarra, ex-bank analyst, agency for 8 clients | desktop | 4/10 (7 with real data) | about 300 TND per client | "Expensive theater until I see real data" |
| Rim, consultant and reseller | phone | 2/10 | 10 TND/month | "Looks like hacker software, not a real tool" |
| Youssef, night-shift courier dispatcher, Sfax | phone | 7/10 | company 200 to 300 TND | "A trader's terminal for deliveries: tells me in seconds who is in trouble" |

## Patterns across the seven
- **Language:** Amel, Rim, Mohamed and Ines all objected to English-only screens or to the unlabelled "Arabizi" draft. Arabic and French are required, not optional.
- **Codes:** Amel, Rim, Mohamed and Karim were confused by ACT / WATCH / CLEAR and by ORD, MSG, F1 to F10. Rim proposed URGENT, CHECKING, SAFE TO SHIP. Plain-word tiers beat clever vocabulary for sellers.
- **The terminal look polarises:** Karim, Sarra and Youssef read it as serious, fast and trustworthy; Amel, Rim, Ines and Mohamed read it as cold, dated or intimidating.
- **Device:** five prefer the phone (Amel, Karim, Mohamed, Rim, Youssef); two prefer desktop (Ines, Sarra). Rim says the phone UI feels like the desktop shrunk.
- **Trust needs real numbers:** Karim and Sarra both objected to permanent "synthetic data" banners and wanted real examples and an audit history (Sarra: 30 days per order).
- **Youssef's asks:** the ACT count as a banner at the top, address-check items coloured by missing, vague or good, and a pre-filled reply box; he found the AI-health metrics too technical.
- **Wanted features:** one-tap send (Karim, Ines), 1/2/3 keys (Ines; they exist in the mock), fewer columns on desktop (Ines), a command bar on the phone (Sarra), a single "Is this risky?" button (Amel).

## Where the personas were wrong or unreliable
- Ines said the desk counters "4 / 1 / 4" were wrong; they match the data.
- Karim said the phone buttons were tiny; the browser check found every phone control at least 44px, so this is about how small the screenshot looked.
- Mohamed's report was a summary, not the requested first-person format.
- Amel read the draft as Arabic; it is Arabizi (Darija in Latin letters). The mock does not label it in plain words.
