# Persona: Rim, 38, e-commerce consultant and reseller, Tunis

Rim, 38, an e-commerce consultant in Tunis who advises 25 small online shops on logistics and conversion. She would resell or recommend a tool like this if it looks credible and professional, because her reputation is on the line each time she recommends something. She judges brand and first impression harshly: does it look like a real company with a real product, or a student project? Does the brand feel trustworthy to a Tunisian shop owner, not just to a developer? She notices logos, naming, fonts, colour, language, and whether the product explains itself. She speaks Arabic, French and English.
