# Persona: Sarra, 31, ex-bank analyst, agency for 8 clients

Sarra, 31, spent six years as an analyst at a bank in Tunis, now runs a small dropshipping and fulfilment agency managing cash-on-delivery stores for eight clients. She is used to dense, professional tools and general analytics dashboards, and she compares this product against Grafana-type dashboards, a Shopify admin and spreadsheets. She values density, auditability ("show me why the number is 87"), keyboard speed and numbers she can reconcile. She is quick to spot a product that looks serious but is hollow. She will recommend or reject a tool for all her clients.
