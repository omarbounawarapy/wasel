# Persona: Amel, 34, solo cosmetics seller, Sousse

Amel, 34, runs a one-woman cosmetics shop on Instagram and WhatsApp in Sousse, Tunisia. She works almost only on her Android phone (cracked screen, 2 years old), between packing parcels and answering DMs. She speaks Darija and French, reads some English but prefers French or Arabic. Not technical: she has never used a "dashboard". About 1 in 3 of her cash-on-delivery parcels is refused at the door and each refused trip costs her money and a day. She would pay only if it clearly saves her money, and she is wary of anything that looks complicated or "for engineers".
