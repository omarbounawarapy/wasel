# Persona: Mohamed, 58, older shop owner, Kairouan

Mohamed, 58, ran a family clothing and household-goods shop in Kairouan for 30 years and now sells online through his nephew's Facebook page. He reads Arabic and French comfortably, English poorly. He wears reading glasses and has trouble with small, low-contrast text, especially in the evening. He is cautious and sceptical of technology: "if I can't read it, I can't trust it". He wants big, calm, clear text and a plain statement of what to do. Refused parcels upset him personally; a customer who refuses is, to him, a matter of respect and reputation. His nephew set up his phone; he mostly uses it at the shop counter.
