# Persona: Karim, 41, electronics shop owner, Tunis

Karim, 41, owns an electronics and phone-accessories shop in Tunis with an online side (Facebook shop, WhatsApp orders, cash on delivery). He has two employees. He uses a laptop in the shop and his phone outside. He is practical, numbers-driven and price-sensitive: "show me the dinars". Orders are 100 to 450 TND, so each refused parcel hurts. He has tried two apps before and dropped them because they were slow to learn. He reads French and English fine. He judges a product in under a minute by whether it looks serious enough to trust with his money.
