Wasel: six premium directions

Each direction changes the whole product feel (layout, type, colour, logo), not just the paint. Same four orders in every screen so you can compare. Not final art: fonts load from Google Fonts, the wordmark is set in the direction's type.

01 Quiet Glass02 Graphite Pro03 Paper & Ink04 Instrument05 Midnight Luxe06 Swiss Mono
01

Quiet Glass

Apple-direction: calm, spacious, translucent

Design idea: treat Wasel like a macOS app. A frosted sidebar, one big sentence as the page title, rows with room to breathe, and colour only where meaning lives (the risk chips). The logo becomes an app-icon tile with a soft gradient and a glass highlight, so it feels like software you install, not a website.

02

Graphite Pro

Dense, dark, keyboard-first tool for daily operators

Design idea: the tool a professional lives in all day. Near-black canvas, hairline borders, tabular numbers, a command bar and visible keyboard shortcuts. Teal is the single signal colour. The mark is an outline arch with a lit parcel, drawn like a pro-app glyph.

03

Paper & Ink

Editorial and calm, like a quality newspaper brief

Design idea: a daily briefing, not a dashboard. Warm paper, a serif headline written as a sentence, a numbered ledger with hairlines, and one oxblood accent for the stamp line. It signals care and judgement, which is what a risk adviser sells. The mark is a printed-ink arch with a red ground rule.

04

Instrument

Tactile and precise, like a well-made measuring device

Design idea: lean into the fact that Wasel is a measuring instrument. A real dial reads the highest-risk order, small dials replace pills, a slim icon rail replaces the sidebar. Warm grey hardware tones, one signal orange. The mark is the arch on a dial face with tick marks.

05

Midnight Luxe

Private-bank feel: dark navy, gold hairlines

Design idea: price the product like a professional service. Deep navy canvas, fine gold hairlines, wide-tracked caps, three large numbers up front and a quiet table below. The mark is a gold line-drawn arch in a navy tile, like an engraved seal.

06

Swiss Mono

Ultra-minimal, black and white, risk as the only colour

Design idea: remove everything that is not information. A huge number states the situation, rows are a strict grid, and the only colour in the whole product is the risk. The mark is a single solid black arch with a cut-out parcel: one shape, no gradient, works on a stamp.
