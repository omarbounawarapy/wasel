Wasel: 20 logo directions

Rough vector sketches, not final art. Each card shows the idea, the lockup with the Arabic name واصل, then the mark at 32px, 16px and 32px on navy to test small-size and reversed legibility. The wordmark is a stand-in font.

What the research said

Build a system, not one fileBrands now ship a full logo, stacked, icon and monogram. So every idea here has to work as a mark alone.
Favicon testClear silhouette, minimal inner detail, strong contrast, no thin strokes. Look at the 16px column.
Arabic and Latin balanceLatin letters take vertical space, Arabic connects horizontally. Keep each script independent in the lockup.
Delivery jobSpeed, reliability and professionalism at a glance. Wasel adds one more: a risk judgement, so it should not promise "safe".
01

Waw route

Waselواصل

Arabic و (first letter of واصل) drawn as one route that ends at a stop.

Brief: Geometric circle bowl + a single tail stroke curling left, ending in a teal dot. One idea, flat, two colours, readable at 16px.
