Wasel: logo refinement round

14 variations of the four shortlisted directions. Each card shows the lockup, then the mark at 48, 32 and 16px, reversed on navy, and in one colour. The waw family also has a "9-test": the mark next to Latin digits and next to the Arabic name, to see how it reads in each context. Wordmark is a stand-in font.
