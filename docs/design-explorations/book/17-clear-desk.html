How this maps to the real project

  • Same pages, same data. Orders to check, WhatsApp messages, Add an order, Money saved, Activity log and the AI-health pages keep their real copy and their real data.
  • What it keeps from Terminal Desk. The rigid panel grid with hairline rules, dense tables, monospaced numbers, the counts strip, the shortcut row on desktop and the one-keystroke actions.
  • What it takes from Review Desk. A light, calm surface, the queue on the left beside the case, filter pills and cards that separate the evidence from the score.
  • What changed after the client rounds. No codes on screen, tier words that name the action (Hold, Ask, Ship), sentences instead of abbreviations, a highlighted word always explained, one primary button per screen, the synthetic-data notice shown once, dark as a switch.
  • Codebase impact. A panel grid component, a tier chip, theme tokens for light and dark, i18n with right-to-left layout, and a global search. The API is unchanged.
  • Honest cost. Less distinctive than the pure terminal, and it needs more width for sentences. French and Arabic cover the list screens only.
