How this maps to the real project

  • Same pages, same copy. Orders to check, WhatsApp messages, Add an order, Money saved, Activity log and the AI-health pages keep their real titles and text.
  • What changes. The orders table becomes a queue plus a case view; the order page becomes an evidence page; the three stat cards become one segmented control.
  • Codebase impact. New queue and case components, evidence cards, a decision bar, J/K keyboard handling. The API is unchanged.
  • Honest cost. It looks like every payments dashboard; the distinctiveness rests on the evidence cards and the customer’s quoted words. Dense queues need the segmented filter to stay useful above about 50 orders.
